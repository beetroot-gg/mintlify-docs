As a member of the team, you have the responsibility to represent the community fairly, professionally and impartially. Read all the information and rules below carefully — knowing them is mandatory and breaking them leads to sanctions.

<Warning>
  This page is intended exclusively for **Beetroot staff team members**. The information presented here is confidential and represents the official conduct guide and internal rules of the staff. If you are a regular player and you have a problem, please contact the staff through the official channels on [Discord](https://discord.beetroot.gg).
</Warning>

---

## General information

<Note>
  **The warn system:** Every staff member can accumulate a maximum of **2.5/3** warns. On reaching the **3/3** threshold, the staff member loses their rank or is downgraded. In serious cases, depending on the situation, they can receive a staff **blacklist**.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Warn expiry:** A warn expires automatically **30 days** after it is issued.
</Note>

**Verbal warnings** are an informal way of sanctioning and serve to warn the staff member about possible mistakes, without adding points in the warn system. However, repeating the same mistake after a verbal warning can lead to the sanction being escalated.

<Note>
  **The administration rule does not apply to staff:** The rule *"The server administration can make decisions outside the rules"* **does not apply** to staff members. They are strictly required to sanction only according to the rules, except where they receive instructions to the contrary from the administration.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Confidentiality:** The information in the **STAFF** category on Discord is **strictly confidential**. Do not share it with players or people outside the team in any form — text, video or photo.
</Note>

**Checks and screenshares are NOT carried out by staff members.** Report these cases in the `#discutii-dovezi` channel.

<Note>
  **Cooldown on returning:** Staff members who step down or are removed **do not have the right to return to the staff** for the next **14 days**.
</Note>

**A fellow staff member breaking the rules:** Gather evidence and speak directly with an **Owner**.

**Abuse by a fellow staff member:** If you hold a rank that allows access to `/ban`, you can ban them to prevent the abuse from continuing, after which you immediately notify the administration.

**Problems with a player:** Contact a member of the administration for support.
