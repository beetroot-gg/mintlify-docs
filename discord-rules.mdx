To keep the community friendly and orderly, all members must follow the rules below. Breaking them leads to sanctions applied by the staff team. Please read them carefully before taking an active part in the channels.

---

## Rules
<Card title="01. Promoting other servers is forbidden" icon="shield-exclamation">

  Promoting other servers, communities or external projects in any channel of our Discord server is completely forbidden.

  **Sanction: Permanent Ban**
</Card>

<Card title="02. Indecent or pornographic images are forbidden" icon="shield-exclamation">

Any image with adult content, pornographic material or explicit/disgusting visual content is forbidden in any channel.

**Sanction:** Timeout 1 day
</Card>

<Card title="03. Sharing images of people without their consent" icon="shield-exclamation">

  Sharing images of real people without their explicit consent is a serious breach of privacy and there is zero tolerance for it on our server.

  **Sanction: Permanent Ban**
</Card>

<Card title="04. Mentioning staff without a concrete reason" icon="shield-exclamation">

Do not tag staff members without a concrete problem or a legitimate question.

**Sanction:** Timeout 1 hour
</Card>

<Card title="05. Vulgar language used to annoy or offend" icon="shield-exclamation">

Occasional swearing can be tolerated within certain limits, but verbal harassment or repeated personal attacks will not be accepted.

**Sanction:** Timeout 1 day
</Card>

<Card title="06. Off-topic discussions in the wrong channels" icon="shield-exclamation">

Every channel has a clear purpose. Discussions that do not fit there must be moved to the general discussion channels.

**Sanction:** Timeout 1 hour
</Card>

<Card title="07. Sharing screen shares from other servers" icon="shield-exclamation">

Actively or implicitly promoting other Minecraft communities by sharing gameplay sessions on other servers is forbidden.

**Sanction:** Timeout 1 day

---


</Card>
