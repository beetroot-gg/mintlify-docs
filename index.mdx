Welcome to the official rules page of the **beetroot.gg** Minecraft server. This document contains all the rules and standards of conduct that every player is required to know and follow. By connecting to the server, you agree to follow these rules in full. Please read all sections carefully before you start playing.

<Warning>
  **Not knowing the rules is not an excuse for breaking them.** All players are responsible for informing themselves about the rules in force. Sanctions apply regardless of whether the player claims they have not read the rules.
</Warning>
