This page contains the fundamental rules of the **beetroot.gg** server, applicable to **all players**, regardless of the rank they hold or the packages they have purchased from the server store. Read them carefully before you connect — failing to follow them can lead to sanctions.

---

## General rules

<Steps>

  <Step title="The rules are universal">
    The rules apply to all players on the **beetroot.gg** server, regardless of the package or rank purchased from the server store.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Following the rules is mandatory">
    Read and follow these rules. Failing to follow them can lead to sanctions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Staff decisions">
    <Note>
      Decisions made by staff are, in general, final. If you believe you have been sanctioned by mistake or unfairly, you can open a **ticket on the Discord server** for clarification or review by a senior staff member.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ignorance is not an excuse">
    <Warning>
      Excuses such as "I didn't know the rule" or "I didn't read the rules" **will not be considered valid** when you break the rules.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Circumventing the rules does not grant immunity">
    <Warning>
      Attempting to "circumvent" the rules **does not grant you immunity** from sanctions.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Proof that you read the rules" iconType="brands">
    To the question "**Have you read the rules?**" the answer is `0753847648`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Password security">
    <Warning>
      Do not use the same password from the **beetroot.gg** server on other platforms (other servers, websites or applications). For the security of your account, it is recommended that you have unique passwords for each service.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complaints against staff members">
    <Note>
      If you believe a staff member is abusing their powers, you can open a **ticket on Discord**, and you need clear evidence to support the complaint.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="The Administration's right to intervene">
    <Note>
      The server Administration (Owner, Operator, Manager) reserves the right to intervene in situations that are not explicitly mentioned in the rules, **only if** that behavior seriously affects other players' gameplay experience or the proper functioning of the server, with the obligation to be able to justify the decision made.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Updating the rules">
    <Note>
      The rules may be updated at any time, but you will be notified on **Discord** when this happens.
    </Note>
  </Step>

</Steps>
