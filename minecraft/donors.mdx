This page sets out the special rules that apply to players who have purchased packages from **store.beetroot.gg**. Owning a package comes with extra perks, but also with matching responsibilities. Breaking the rules below can lead to the partial or total loss of your privileges, as well as additional sanctions.

<Note>
  Donors are subject to **all the general rules** of the Beetroot server, regardless of the package they own. Purchased perks do not grant immunity from the general rules and are not an excuse for inappropriate behavior.
</Note>

---

## Additional rules for donors

<Card title="Abusing perks" icon="shield-exclamation">

You are not allowed to use donor perks to ruin other players' experience (for example, using the `/fly` command to flee from PvP).

  **Sanction:** Depending on severity

</Card>

<Card title="Toxicity and vulgar language on Donor Chat" icon="shield-exclamation">

Excessive vulgar language and toxicity used repeatedly on the donor chat (`/dc`) will be sanctioned more harshly.

  **Sanction:** Removal of access to `/dc` (Donor Chat)

</Card>

<Card title="Breaking the general rules" icon="shield-exclamation">

Donors are not allowed to repeatedly break the server's general rules.

  **Sanction:** Depending on severity

</Card>

<Card title="Selling or sharing accounts" icon="shield-exclamation">

Donors are strictly forbidden from selling or sharing their accounts with other players.

  **Sanction: Permanent Ban & Warn 3/3**

</Card>
