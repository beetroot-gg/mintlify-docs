The rules below apply across the entire **Beetroot** server: in public chat, donor chat, private messages, `/helpop`, `/eac`, item names, signs, gang names and in any other form of communication.

<Note>
  **Romanian** and **English** are permitted. Repeated offenses or serious cases can attract heavier sanctions.
</Note>

## Language and behavior

<Card title="01. Foreign languages" icon="language">

Repeatedly using phrases or sentences in languages other than Romanian and English is forbidden.

**Sanction:** `Mute 30 minutes`
</Card>

<Card title="02. Insults and abuse" icon="message-slash">

You must not insult, swear at, threaten or use vulgar language towards anyone. Abbreviations, censored words and expressions written in another language, such as `plm` or `fmm`, are sanctioned as well.

- **Public chat or donor chat:** `Mute 2 hours`
- **Items, signs, gang, /msg, /helpop, etc.:** `Warn`
</Card>

<Card title="03. Toxicity" icon="comments">

Tasteless jokes, irony, sarcasm or expressions used to disparage another player or to harm their experience are not permitted.

- **Public chat or donor chat:** `Mute 2 hours`
- **Items, signs, gang, /msg, /helpop, etc.:** `Warn`
</Card>

<Card title="04. Excessive toxicity" icon="triangle-exclamation">

Behavior becomes excessive toxicity when the offense is repeated or continues after several sanctions have been applied.

Examples:

- you continue the toxic comments after being sanctioned;
- you try to evade the sanction through irony or indirect insults;
- you keep a hostile attitude after repeated warnings.

**Sanction:** depending on severity, `Suspension of access to /donorchat for an indefinite period`, `Mute 12 hours` or `Ban 3–7 days`
</Card>

<Card title="05. Racism" icon="ban">

Any form of racism, direct or indirect, is strictly forbidden.

- **In chat:** `Mute 3 days`
- **In gangs, item names or builds:** `Ban 3 days`
</Card>

<Card title="06. Insulting the staff or the server" icon="shield-halved">

Insults, disrespect, irony and tasteless jokes aimed at a staff member or at the server are forbidden.

**Sanction:** `Mute 3 days`
</Card>

## Using the chat

<Card title="07. Spam" icon="repeat">

Do not send the same message more than twice in a row. Different variants of the same message, used to repeat the same idea, also count as spam.

**Sanction:** `Mute 1 hour`
</Card>

<Card title="08. Caps Lock" icon="font">

Excessive use of capital letters is forbidden. Messages written predominantly in capitals are considered abusive CAPS LOCK.

**Sanction:** `Mute 1 hour`
</Card>

<Card title="09. Stretching words" icon="text-width">

Excessively stretching words or sending meaningless messages, such as `saluuuuuut`, `haaaaaai` or `asdfghjkl`, is forbidden.

**Sanction:** `Mute 1 hour`
</Card>

<Card title="10. Asking for favors" icon="hand">

You must not ask staff members for favors, items or advantages.

**Sanction:** `Warn`
</Card>

<Card title="11. Hack accusations" icon="user-secret">

Do not accuse a player of hacking in public chat. Record the proof and report the player on Discord.

**Sanction:** `Warn`
</Card>

<Card title="12. Communicating with the staff" icon="headset">

All the rules on this page also apply in `/helpop`, `/eac` and in private messages sent to staff members.

**Sanction:** `Warn`
</Card>

## Advertising and safety

<Card title="13. Disclosing personal data" icon="user-shield">
  <Warning>
  You must not disclose a person's identity, location, IP address, phone number, address or other personal data.

  **Sanction:** `Permanent Ban`
  </Warning>
</Card>

<Card title="14. Indirect advertising" icon="bullhorn">

Messages that suggest or look for other servers are forbidden. Examples: _"who has a server?"_, _"looking for a server"_ or _"who has played on server X?"_.

**Sanction:** `Mute 7 days`
</Card>

<Card title="15. Promoting external communities" icon="rectangle-ad">
  <Warning>
  Promoting or mentioning communities, servers or projects unrelated to Beetroot is forbidden. This rule covers Minecraft servers, other games and Discord servers.

  **Sanction:** `Permanent Ban`
  </Warning>
</Card>

<Card title="16. Promoting personal accounts" icon="share-nodes">

Promoting personal TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or other platform accounts is forbidden, both in public chat and in private messages.

**Sanction:** `Mute 3 days` / `Warn`
</Card>
