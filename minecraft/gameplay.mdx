This page contains the gameplay rules that apply on the **Beetroot** server (beetroot.gg). Every player is required to read, understand and follow these rules. Breaking them can lead to temporary or permanent sanctions, including the loss of your items, money and builds.

---

## Cheating and hacking

<Card title="Using hacks" icon="shield-exclamation">

<Warning>
  Using mods, programs or tools that give you an unfair advantage is **strictly forbidden**. You may not use hacks, autoclickers, macros, PvP mods such as BetterPvP, or any other kind of software that gives you an advantage over other players. Players are not allowed to use two attack inputs at the same time. You may not place objects on your keyboard/mouse in order to automate actions such as mining, farming, fishing, etc. (including F3+T). If you break this rule, you risk sanctions on every account you own, including the loss of your items, money and builds.

  **Sanction: Permanent Ban**
</Warning>

</Card>

<Card title="Farming using forbidden methods" icon="shield-exclamation">

You may not use the methods listed above (hacks, autoclickers, macros, etc.) to farm resources or progress on the server.

**Sanction:** `Ban 14 days`

---

</Card>

<Card title="Butterfly, Dragclick and Jitter" icon="shield-exclamation">

Clicking methods such as **butterfly**, **dragclick** and **jitter** are not recommended, because they will mislead the anticheat.

**Sanction:** `Ban 15 minutes` *(issued by the anticheat)*

---

</Card>

<Card title="Using the Schematica Printer" icon="shield-exclamation">

You may not use the **Schematica printer** to build on the server.

**Sanction:** `Ban 30 days` + build deleted

---

</Card>

<Card title="Using X-Ray" icon="shield-exclamation">

You may not use **X-Ray mods or textures**.

**Sanction:** `Ban 30 days`

---

</Card>

<Card title="Pretending to use hacks" icon="shield-exclamation">

<Warning>
  You may not **pretend** to use hacks in order to mislead a staff member.

  **Sanction: Permanent Ban**
</Warning>

---

</Card>

<Card title="Helping players who cheat" icon="shield-exclamation">

<Warning>
  You may not play alongside a "hacker" and you are required to report them on Discord at **#tickets**.

  **Sanction:** Ban 30 days
</Warning>

**Examples of scenarios that count as complicity:**

- You work together with a player who uses hacks to eliminate another player.
- You send a friend to farm with an autoclicker at a farm or to collect spawners with X-Ray.
- You give or receive items on alt accounts in order to benefit from advantages obtained dishonestly.
- You play alongside a "hacker" and do not report them.

---

</Card>

## Screenshare check

You can be taken to a screenshare check if you are suspected of using hacks or other forbidden methods. You will be sanctioned after the check in any of the following situations:

<Warning>
  - You admit that you used hacks before the check.
  - It is found that you made changes to your mouse software before the check.
  - You cannot share your screen or complete the echo/ocean check for any reason.
  - You empty the recycle bin or delete files from your PC before the check.
  - You leave the game during the check (even if you claim reasons such as "my internet dropped, I crashed or the power went out").
  - You disconnect/reconnect the USB or the mouse before the check.
  - You refuse to do the check.
  - Hacks or forbidden mods are found during the check.
</Warning>

Exploiting bugs is **strictly forbidden**. You are required to report any bug you discover on Discord at **#tickets**.

| Bug Type | Sanction |
|---|---|
| Pearl bug | Ban 3 days |
| Combat disconnect bug | Ban 7 days |
| Elytra bug | Ban 7 days |
| Block-climbing bug | Ban 3 days |
| Hitting through blocks bug | Ban 3 days |

## Griefing

<Card title="Griefing in another player's area" icon="shield-exclamation">

You may not grief near a player's claim, on their claim or on their island. This includes intentional modification (destruction, abusive builds, block spam, placing lava/water, etc.) in a way that negatively affects another player's base, island or protected area.

**Sanction:** `Ban 30 days`

---

</Card>

<Card title="Incitement" icon="shield-exclamation">

You may not encourage other players to do things that can get them sanctioned.

**Sanction:** `Ban 7 days`

---

</Card>

<Card title="Harassing staff members" icon="shield-exclamation">

<Warning>
  Trying to "hunt" the server staff in order to make them slip up is strictly forbidden.

  **Sanction: Permanent Ban**
</Warning>

---

</Card>

## Profileboosting

<Warning>
  **Profileboosting is strictly forbidden.** Profileboosting means using or creating multiple accounts in order to benefit a main account.

  **Examples of profileboosting:**
  - Using `/rewards`-type commands on multiple accounts in order to transfer them.
  - Creating accounts in order to claim rewards, kits or free benefits.
  - Creating accounts to complete daily quests / battlepass.
  - Creating accounts in order to assign them islands on which generators are placed.

  **Sanction:** A ban on the accounts involved, depending on severity, and disqualification from the leaderboards.
</Warning>
