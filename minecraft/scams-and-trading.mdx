This page covers the rules on trading, transactions and scamming on **Beetroot**. To keep the game environment fair and safe, any form of deception or illegal transaction is strictly forbidden and sanctioned. Read the sections below carefully before you make any exchange with other players.

---

## Prohibitions

The following actions are strictly forbidden on the server. Serious violations are sanctioned with a Permanent Ban, regardless of context or intent.

<Card title="Out-of-game deals" icon="shield-exclamation">

You may not make deals outside the game (e.g. *"selling a Nightmare sword for 10 euros via PayPal"*).

  **Sanction: Permanent Ban**

</Card>

<Card title="Selling accounts" icon="shield-exclamation">

You may not sell accounts.

  **Sanction: Permanent Ban**

</Card>

<Card title="Sharing accounts" icon="shield-exclamation">

You may not share your account with other players. The shared account will be banned.

  **Sanction: Permanent Ban**

</Card>

<Card title="Faking evidence" icon="shield-exclamation">

You may not fake evidence of any kind.

  **Sanction: Permanent Ban**

</Card>

<Card title="Scamming on /ah or through /trade" icon="shield-exclamation">

Scamming on `/ah` or through `/trade` using renamed items (spawners, heads, etc.) or shulkers with fake contents in order to mislead is forbidden.

  **Sanction:** 7-day Ban

</Card>

<Card title="Casino-style builds" icon="shield-exclamation">

You may not make casino / slot machine / shell game builds or anything similar.

  **Sanction:** 7-day Ban + build deleted

</Card>

<Card title="Deceiving players to obtain items" icon="shield-exclamation">

You may not lie to or trick other players in order to obtain items or advantages.

  **Sanction:** Depending on severity

---


</Card>

## Safe trades

The server's administration and staff **are not responsible** for trades made without a middleman. The server team also takes no responsibility for access you voluntarily give other players to protections, islands, items or other in-game property, or for the players you choose to ally with. If you decided to give someone access to your property, staff cannot intervene afterwards. However, if a player deceived or manipulated you in order to gain access to your property, they will be sanctioned according to the rules.

<Note>
  If you make a trade **without a middleman** and you fall victim to a scam, staff **cannot offer you support**. To be protected in case of a dispute, always use the server's official middleman system.
</Note>

<Tip>
  To keep your transactions safe, use the **official middleman**, available by opening a ticket on our Discord server: [discord.beetroot.gg](https://discord.beetroot.gg)
</Tip>
