Welcome to the general information page of the **Beetroot** server! Here you find answers to the most frequent questions from our players. We recommend you go through this page before opening a ticket on Discord, so you can quickly find the information you need.

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="Where do I find the server rules?">
    The server rules can be found at: [rules.beetroot.gg](https://rules.beetroot.gg/)

    Reading them is **recommended for all players**, to avoid various possible problems during your gameplay.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I report a hacker?">
    If you suspect that a player is using external applications that give an in-game advantage, you can open a **ticket on the Discord server** in the `#tickets` category.

    <Warning>
      **Mandatory conditions for reporting:**
      - To open a ticket you must have concrete proof.
      - The proof must be recorded as clearly as possible.
      - The proof must be uploaded to **YouTube**.
      - Proof recorded with a phone is **not taken into consideration**, and the ticket will be automatically rejected.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I make a suggestion for the server?">
    Do you have ideas and would you like to see them implemented on the server? You can make a suggestion to the owners on **Discord** in the `#suggestions` category.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I buy from the store?">
    Payments can be made through the following methods:

    - 💳 **Bank Card**
    - 🎟️ **PaysafeCard**
    - 🪙 **Crypto**
    - 🅿️ **PayPal** (by contacting `coiul.` on Discord)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are Beets?">
    **Beets** are the server's global currency. Beets can be purchased from the store and can be used in the `/points` menu in game.

    With Beets you can buy:
    - 🗝️ Keys
    - 📚 Books
    - 🧿 Talismans
    - 🏭 Spawners
    - 🐾 Pets

    <Warning>
      **Important:**
      - Beets **DO NOT disappear** when a section or the entire server is wiped.
      - You can trade with other players using Beets. (`/trade` | `#tickets`)
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I join the staff team?">
    To apply for the staff team, open a ticket on **Discord** in the [#👑・staff-application](LINK_STAFF_APPLICATION_CHANNEL) category.

    **Required conditions:**
    - At least **14 years old**
    - No sanctions in the last **2 weeks**
    - At least **48 hours** played on the server
    - Knowledge of Romanian/English and its grammar
    - A properly completed application
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I vote for the server?">
    <Tip>
      By voting for the server you help the community grow and you receive in-game rewards!
    </Tip>

    - Type `/vote` in chat and in the menu shown you will find the buttons with the voting sites.
    - Your in-game name must be **spelled correctly** on the site.
    - You must be **online** while you vote in order to receive the rewards.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you think you were sanctioned wrongly?">
    - Open a **ticket on Discord** and avoid causing a scene in chat.
    - If the staff member does not have proof, they will be sanctioned, and your sanction will be canceled.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I play on the Beetroot server from my phone?">
    Yes! The server is also accessible from mobile devices:

    - 📱 **Pojav Launcher** *(Play Store)* — IP: `beetroot.gg`
    - 🟢 **Minecraft Bedrock** — IP: `bedrock.beetroot.gg`
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Found a bug?">
    <Tip>
      Bugs can be **rewarded** depending on how serious they are!
    </Tip>

    - Report it in a **ticket on Discord**!
    - Bugs are **NOT** announced in the global chat.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When are events organized?">
    - All events will be announced on **Discord** at least **12 hours** in advance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you want to trade with someone?">
    - **Trade with real money on the store:** open a ticket on Discord under `Trade Store`.
    - **Trade without real money:** open a ticket on Discord under `Intermediere`.

    <Warning>
      **Important:**
      - We are **NOT** responsible for trades made without an intermediary.
      - You are **NOT** allowed to sell items for real money outside our store.

      Failure to comply can lead to a **network-wide ban**, for an unlimited period.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can't find the information you are looking for?">
    - Join our **Discord** server where you can ask questions or open a ticket!
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I become media?">
    - You can apply for Media on **Discord**, in the [#📹・media-application](LINK_MEDIA_APPLICATION_CHANNEL) channel.
      - Make sure you **meet the conditions** mentioned in that channel.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I contact you?">
    - You can contact us at the e-mail address [support@beetroot.gg](mailto:support@beetroot.gg) or on [Discord](https://discord.beetroot.gg).
  </Accordion>
