This **Privacy Policy** explains how **Beetroot** collects, uses and protects the personal information you may provide when you access and use our online store for the digital products intended for the Minecraft server.

---

1. **Information collected**

When you make purchases in our online store, we ask you to provide your name, e-mail address and billing address.

This information is mandatory for issuing the fiscal invoice in accordance with Romanian legislation.

2. **Legal basis of the processing**

We process your personal data mainly for:

• performance of the contract (processing and delivery of the order);

• compliance with legal obligations (invoicing and fiscal records);

• our legitimate interest (account security, fraud prevention).

3. **Use of the information**

The personal information we collect is used mainly to process orders and to provide you with the digital products you have purchased.

We may also use it for:

• sending updates regarding orders and invoices;

• communications related to the operation of the services and technical support.

4. **Cookies and similar technologies**

The site may use cookies and similar technologies for the correct operation of the store and, where applicable, for traffic statistics.

You can control the use of cookies from your browser settings.

5. **Protection of the information**

We undertake to protect our customers' personal information and we take appropriate measures to ensure its security.

All payment information is processed using secure methods.

Customers' payment data is secured by the Stripe site (and, where applicable, other payment processors), which complies with high security and confidentiality standards.

For more details, please consult Stripe's privacy policy.

6. **Sharing of the information**

We will not sell, distribute or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties, except for:

• situations provided for by law;

• service providers necessary for the operation of the store (e.g.: payment processors, hosting providers), who process the data on our behalf.

7. **Storage period**

Data related to orders and invoicing is kept for the period required by the applicable fiscal legislation.

Other data is kept only for as long as it is necessary for the purposes mentioned above or until the right to erasure is exercised, where this is possible.

8. **Your rights**

You can request access to the data collected about you, its correction or the erasure of non-essential data (within the limits of fiscal law).

Contact us by email from the site for these requests. We respond in accordance with the GDPR.

Note: Data from invoices and Stripe/Skrill transactions is kept for 10 years in accordance with Romanian fiscal law.

9. **Updates to the Privacy Policy**

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time.

Any changes will be published on this page and will take effect from the date of publication.

10. **Contact**

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us through the contact information available on our site.
